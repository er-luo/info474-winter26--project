Cherry Blossoms in Seattle
Every spring, Seattle transforms into a city of pink petals.Visitors gather to see the blooms while longtime residents anticipate the season’s arrival. But cherry blossoms are more than a short-lived spectacle - their presence reflects decades of planting, preservation, and change.

By tracing where Seattle’s cherry blossoms grow and how long they have been part of the landscape, this article explores how beauty, history, and place intersect across the city.

Density Map of Cherry Blossoms Across Major Cities in the Greater Seattle Area
Cherry blossoms are not evenly spread across Seattle. Some neighborhoods have far more trees than others, reflecting different planting histories, street designs, and maintenance patterns across the city. This density map highlights where cherry blossoms appear most frequently, revealing clusters of blossoms and areas where they are less common. By looking at these patterns, we can begin to see how cherry blossoms are woven into Seattle’s urban landscape.
